# Keep Persepolis's rules, but not its defects

"Exactly the same" means Swiftpolis reproduces the rules of Persepolis's `test` branch at `8940bcf`, such as one active Download per Queue and five Passes, while the defects in [upstream-defects.md](../upstream-defects.md) are fixed. Any other behaviour change must be documented first. We rejected a literal copy because some defects stop features working at all, such as main-window Pause and cookies from the browser extension.
